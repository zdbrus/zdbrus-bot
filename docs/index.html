Zdbrus Bot

The free auto-play bot for Firestone: Idle Clicker Online RPG. It runs inside the game, so it never misclicks, at any screen size.

Install in 4 steps

  1. Install MelonLoader on Firestone (point the installer at Firestone.exe).
  2. Start the game once and close it.
  3. Extract ZdbrusBot.zip into the Firestone folder (Steam: right-click Firestone, Manage, Browse local files).
  4. Start the game, press F1 to choose features, then F7 to start.

What it plays for you

Daily routine

Daily rewards, check-in, mail, quests, free shop items and battle pass rewards.

Research

Firestone and meteorite research in your priority order, plus forbidden knowledge.

Guild

Expeditions, free pickaxes, guild crystal, Chaos Rift, personal tree and hero awakening.

Map and Warfront

Map missions with free speedups, Warfront loot and Liberation missions back to back.

Arena and war machines

Fights the easiest opponent, uses free refreshes, levels the war machine you pick.

Town and events

Alchemist, Oracle, tavern, exotic merchant, Scarab's game and seasonal event shops.

Collector

Clicks coal miners and double-beer dragons the moment they show up.

Never spends gems

Every purchase needs the button enabled and enough of the exact currency. No gems, no real money, ever.
