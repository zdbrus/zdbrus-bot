# War Machine Guide

Plain-language war machine tips for Zdbrus Bot players, gathered from community guides (see [Sources](#sources)). In game, press **F4** and scroll to **War machine tier list** to see where your own machines rank.

Game updates can change numbers, so treat this as a guide, not gospel.

## The short version

- **Only jewel chests matter for war machines.** Jewel chests (Wooden, Iron, Golden, Diamond, Opal, Emerald, Platinum) give war machine parts (screws, cogs and metal) and the arcane conduits that unlock new machines. Gear chests (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic) don't.
- **You can't pick which machine unlocks next.** The first five come in a set order of roles; after that it's random.
- **Campaign: pick one main machine and always level it.** Which one matters less than you'd think.
- **Blueprints: damage first, then health**, on machines you'll keep (S or A tier). Go easy on blueprints for B and C tier machines, because blueprints are lost if you replace the machine later.
- **Levels and rarity on any machine help all of them**, so they're never wasted.
- **Never buy Opal chests with expedition tokens.** Buy Diamond chests and transmute them instead.

## Getting started

- War machines open up at **character level 50**, through the Engineer in town. Do the short tutorial first.
- **Give every machine a crew.** Heroes in a machine's crew boost it a lot. In game: Town → Engineer → Engineer → Active squad (swords) → Edit → pencil. Or press **F4**, click **Auto-build squad**, then **Apply to game**.
- Each machine has **4 crew seats**, 5 at Engineer level 30 and 6 at Engineer level 60.
- **A good start** (from the Japanese wiki): level your first attacker to about 5–15, push campaign missions and open the chests you win, and when your 3rd machine (a tank) arrives, get both to about level 15–20. **40 campaign stars** is the first big goal.

## Chests: jewel, gear and celestial

Firestone has three chest families. Only **jewel chests** help war machines.

| Jewel chest | War machine parts | Arcane conduits (average) | Extras |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Wooden | 11 | 0.14 | 1 jewel |
| Iron | 22 | 0.27 | 2 jewels |
| Golden | 33 | 0.41 | 3 jewels |
| Diamond | 44 | 0.55 | 4 jewels, 1 in 10 chance of a legendary seal |
| Opal | 55 | 0.68 | 5 jewels, legendary and mythic seal chances |
| Emerald | 132 | 1.64 | 6 jewels, mythic and titan seal chances |
| Platinum | 264 | 3.27 | 7 jewels, titan and angel seal chances |

**Where jewel chests come from:**

- War machine campaign missions.
- The **Guild Shop**, paid with expedition tokens.
- The **Exotic Merchant's Emblem Market** (from character level 65), paid with **emblems of valor**. Spend emblems on the best chest you can: Golden, then Diamond once you have 100 campaign stars. Diamond beats Golden while you still need epic jewels or legendary seals.

**How parts are shared out:**

- Parts belong to one machine. Cloudfist's screws can't be used on Talos.
- Chests split parts so your machines end up with about the same total. That means **a newly unlocked machine gets nearly all parts until it catches up** with the others.
- Once you have 6 or more machines you can pick **5 favourites** in the Engineer. Favourites get 80% of parts; the other 20% is shared by everyone.

## How new machines unlock

New machines unlock automatically once you've collected enough **arcane conduits** of all three kinds. Conduits only come from jewel chests.

This is the long view: how good each machine is over time, so you know what to build and what to hope for. The planner's power numbers show what's strongest **right now**; the tier list shows what's worth **investing in**.

The main tiers come from the Japanese player wiki (Sept 2025). The arena column is a second opinion from 2024 arena simulations (1 is best, 4 is worst). The source has no D tier; **C is the lowest**.

**What the tiers mean for your resources:**

- **S and A:** worth building all the way: levels, rarity and blueprints.
- **B:** fine for now. Level it, but go easy on blueprints.
- **C:** spend lightly until something better unlocks.

**Goal squad:** Cloudfist, Talos, Thunderclap, Goliath and Hunter, the best of each kind. Something to work toward as they unlock.

**The two sources disagree on healers.** The wiki rates Hunter S, but the 2024 arena simulations put every healer last and preferred **2 tanks + 3 single-target attackers** (or 2 tanks + 2 single-target + 1 multi-target attacker) for the arena. If arena matters most to you, a second tank may beat a healer.

## Where to spend

### Levels (expedition tokens + that machine's parts)

- Every machine level gives the Engineer 100 XP, and **every Engineer level adds 5% to all your machines**. So leveling any machine is never wasted.
- The planner's **Upgrade advisor** (F4) shows which level gives the most power for your tokens and whether each machine has the parts.

### Rarity (tools)

- Tools are free from the Engineer every 6 hours (225 + 25 × Engineer level) or can be bought in the Guild Shop.
- Each rarity step gives **+5% to that machine, +5% to all your machines, +5% to all machines in the arena, and +3% to that machine's ability chance**. Early rarities are great value.

| Rarity | Tools | Machine level needed |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Uncommon | 10,000 | 10 |
| Rare | 51,000 | 50 |
| Epic | 180,000 | 100 |
| Legendary | 220,000 | 160 |
| Mythic | 280,000 | 230 |
| Titan | 300,000 | 310 |
| Angel | 310,000 | 400 |

- Rarity order for campaign players: main machine → favourites → the rest, then repeat.

### Blueprints

- Blueprints only help the machine you spend them on, so they're what you lose if you bench it later. That's why tiers matter most here.
- **Damage first, then health.** Armor barely helps in campaign fights but does count toward a mission's power requirement, so some armor on your main is fine. In the arena, every stat counts.
- Spare blueprints: build up your arena machines, or machines whose milestone bonuses help the rest of the game (prestige Firestone gain, then gold, then all attributes).

### Campaign or arena?

- **Campaign:** level one main machine and never stop. The Steam guide ranks mains Thunderclap > Cloudfist > Talos, Aegis, Earthshatterer, Judgement, Harvester > Firecracker > Fortress, Goliath > Curator > Hunter, Sentinel, but the gap is small (about 7 levels from top to bottom by campaign star 190). Settle on your main and favourites soon after machine #6; switching later costs a lot of parts.
- **Arena:** level your five favourites evenly, push rarity, and upgrade everything. The guide warns that being competitive in the arena takes real spending.

## Expedition tokens and other tips

- Expedition tokens are shared by **war machine levels** and the **personal tree**. Free players rarely have spare tokens after leveling machines. In the personal tree, only the first few levels of Battle Cry, Gold, Prestigious and Firestone Effect are worth tokens early; the Japanese wiki calls **Battle Cry** the top priority.
- Don't spend expedition tokens on Arcane Crystal pickaxes; claim the free ones instead (the bot's Free Pickaxes task does this).
- Don't buy Opal chests with tokens. Diamond chests transmuted into Opal are cheaper.
- Amulets (Japanese wiki): **Liberation** is the best (5% chance of double daily Liberation mission rewards). **Quartermaster** (+5% war machine campaign loot) and **Pioneers** (5% chance of double war machine campaign loot) are next, but only affect the 6-hourly campaign loot.

## Chest timing trick (new accounts with 3–5 machines)

From the Steam guide (credit to Yakinthos for testing): this got a fresh free account to 40 campaign stars in **14 days instead of 19**.

**Why it works:** a newly unlocked machine soaks up nearly all parts until it catches up. If you open a big pile of chests **just before** a new machine unlocks, those parts go to the machines you already have, so your main gets stronger sooner.

**How:**

1. After your 3rd machine arrives, open only **Wooden** chests. Save every Iron and Golden chest.
2. When you're close to the 4th machine, open Wooden chests carefully until you're **1 conduit short** of the unlock. Then open **50 Iron chests** at once. Level your main as far as you can and push missions.
3. Do the same right before the **5th** machine, this time with **50 Golden chests**.
4. Do the same right before the **6th** machine with **50 Iron chests** again. You should now be able to push to 40 campaign stars.

**Bot note:** the bot's **Open Chests** task opens Wooden, Iron and Golden chests every 20 minutes by default, which would spoil the trick. While you're doing it, untick **Open Wooden Chests**, **Open Iron Chests** and **Open Golden Chests** in **F1 → Daily → Open Chests**, and tick them again once you have 6 machines.
