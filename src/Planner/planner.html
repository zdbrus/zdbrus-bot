Crew bench

Drag a hero into a bay, or tap a hero then tap an empty slot. The gold number is how much that hero would add to the selected machine's power. Tap a hero's name to edit their jewels. Hero faces fill in after you open Engineer, then Select crew, once in game.

Machine math

Every multiplier stacks: base × (1+level) × (1+engineer) × (1+blueprint) × (1+rarity) × (1+card) × (1+inscription) × (1+artifact) × (1+crew). Source: Firestone wiki, game version 9.1.3. Celestial jewel levels 17 and 18 are estimated until we confirm them from your game.

Ethereal shard advisor

Ethereal shards enchant jewels, and jewels on crew heroes are what raise war machine stats. Each row is the next enchant for a hero in your current squad, ranked by squad power gained per 100 shards. Costs follow the game's pattern (60, 120, 240, 480, 960…); the first enchant of a jewel is estimated at 30. Gear (void crystals) and soulstones (soul shards) only raise hero stats, not war machines.
